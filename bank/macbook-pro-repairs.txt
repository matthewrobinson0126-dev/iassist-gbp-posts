We repair every MacBook Pro, from 2010 unibody models to the 2026 M5: screens, batteries, MagSafe and USB-C ports, keyboards, liquid damage and micro-soldering.

Every machine is diagnosed under a microscope, the failed part is named in your quote, and where we can, we repair the part instead of the whole board.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
