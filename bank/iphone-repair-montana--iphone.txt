In Montana, many households run three generations of iPhones at once: a child's first phone, a parent's daily driver and a grandparent's phone set up for easy use.

Don't replace a broken one just yet. We repair iPhone screens, batteries and back glass, and we handle board-level faults too. Our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte is about 60 km away, around 55 to 60 minutes via the N1, so most Montana customers use our courier collection instead.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty.
