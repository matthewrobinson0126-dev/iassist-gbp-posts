We repair every M1 MacBook Pro: the 13-inch and the 14-inch and 16-inch M1 Pro and Max.

Common faults include Touch Bar and USB-C board failures on the 13-inch, display cable failures on the 14- and 16-inch, MagSafe 3 ports, Touch ID after a top case replacement, and liquid damage (the SSD is soldered, so act fast).

Drop it off at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or book our R300 door-to-door collection anywhere in Gauteng. Outside Gauteng, we arrange courier collection nationwide.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
