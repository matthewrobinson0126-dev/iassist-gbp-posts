iPhone glass cracked but the picture underneath still perfect, with no lines or dead patches? You may only need new glass.

We separate the cracked glass with a cold laser and rebond new glass, keeping your original OLED (and True Tone). It's available from the iPhone X onwards. If the display itself is damaged, we'll recommend a screen replacement instead.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
