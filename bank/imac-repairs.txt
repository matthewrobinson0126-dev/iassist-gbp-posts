We repair every iMac, from 2012 Intel models to the latest M-series, including ones iStore no longer services.

Common jobs include failing Fusion Drives, 5K GPU faults on 27-inch models, cracked screens and newer macOS installs with OCLP. Your data comes first, and a repair is often 50 to 80% cheaper than replacing the iMac.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
