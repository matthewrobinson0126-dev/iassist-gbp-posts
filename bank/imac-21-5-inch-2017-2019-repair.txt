The 2017 and 2019 21.5-inch iMac has a gorgeous Retina 4K screen, but the Fusion Drive is often the weak point, and it slows everything down.

Fitting a proper SSD gives these iMacs a second life. Because the RAM slots are hidden behind the display, upgrading memory is not a DIY job. We remove the screen properly, fit the SSD, upgrade the RAM and reseal it with new adhesive.

We can also install a newer macOS with OCLP, service the cooling, and repair power supplies and logic boards.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty.
