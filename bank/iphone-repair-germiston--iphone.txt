Germiston is South Africa's biggest railway junction and a major manufacturing and logistics hub.

Many iPhones here spend the workday in a truck cab, a warehouse or a depot, where drops and dust take their toll. We repair screens, charging ports and batteries, and we fix board-level faults too.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Germiston by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
