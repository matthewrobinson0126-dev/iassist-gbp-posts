The 24-inch iMac is the thinnest iMac Apple has made. Its logic board combines the chip, memory and storage in one unit, so when it fails, most repair shops say no.

We don't. Chip-level board work is what we do. We repair cracked screens, dead ports, liquid damage, sudden no-boot faults and logic board problems on the M1, M3 and M4 models. Liquid damage on these machines needs a different approach, and we have a set process for it.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and a written quote, and carries a 6-month warranty. We repair iMacs from all over South Africa via courier.
