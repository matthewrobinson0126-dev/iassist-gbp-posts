Randpark Ridge sits right next to Randpark Golf Club, home to big events like the Joburg Open and the SA Open.

Golf is hard on iPhones: a fall from a golf cart, a slip out of a bag pocket, chipped back glass from knocking against clubs. We repair screens, back glass, batteries and cameras, and we handle board-level faults too.

Drop your iPhone off at our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or book door-to-door courier collection from R300.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
