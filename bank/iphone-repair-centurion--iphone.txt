Centurion sits on the N1 between Johannesburg and Pretoria, well connected but not well served for complex iPhone repairs.

Drop your iPhone off at our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or book door-to-door courier collection from R300.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
