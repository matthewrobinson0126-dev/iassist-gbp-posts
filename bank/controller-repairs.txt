A new DualSense costs well over a thousand rand. The fault that usually kills one, stick drift, comes down to a part that costs a fraction of that.

At iAssist Repair we fix PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo controllers at component level: drifting sticks, dead bumpers, triggers that stopped pushing back, loose charging ports and batteries that no longer last a session.

Drift repair starts from R350 per stick. Ask about a Hall Effect stick upgrade, which uses magnets instead of worn contacts.

We cover the DualSense and DualSense Edge, DualShock 4, Xbox Series and Xbox One controllers, the Xbox Elite Series 2, Joy-Cons and the Switch Pro Controller. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
