If your iPhone's glass is cracked but the OLED underneath is perfect, refurbishing the screen costs much less than a new one.

We rebond new glass onto your original OLED using a cold laser, from the iPhone 12 onwards, and keep the screen paired, so there's no "unknown part" message, even on the iPhone 15 and later.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect anywhere in Gauteng for a flat R300, insured both ways.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
