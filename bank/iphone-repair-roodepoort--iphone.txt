The Walter Sisulu National Botanical Garden is one of the West Rand's favourite walks. Its rocky trail to the Witpoortjie Waterfall is also a reliable way to drop an iPhone.

A slip on wet rock or a knock against stone while chasing a photo of the falls usually means a cracked screen, back glass or camera lens. We repair all three, along with batteries, charging faults and water damage.

Pop in to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our courier collection from R300 and we'll fetch your iPhone from your door.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
