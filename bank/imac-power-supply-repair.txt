iMac completely dead, shutting off straight after starting, or dead after load shedding, a power surge or a lightning strike? It's often the internal power supply.

We replace iMac power supplies on every model, with prices from R1,200. It's worth trying an SMC reset first; if that doesn't help, we'll diagnose it for free.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or ask us about door-to-door collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
