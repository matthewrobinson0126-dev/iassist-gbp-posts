Is the heart rate reading gone, ECG not working, or Blood Oxygen unavailable? Or is the back glass on your Apple Watch simply cracked?

On an Apple Watch, the back glass and the health sensors are one bonded unit. We replace the whole back-and-sensor assembly and recalibrate the sensors, so the readings work properly again.

Prices depend on your model: from R600 for Series 1 to 3, R800 for Series 4 and 5, R900 for SE and SE 2, and up to R1,500 to R1,800 for Ultra models with their larger sapphire back.

We repair every model from Series 1 through Ultra 3 and Series 11. Each job starts with a free diagnostic.
