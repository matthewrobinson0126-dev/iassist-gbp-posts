Benoni is known as the City of Lakes, with spots like Homestead Lake and the Korsman Bird Sanctuary.

That lakeside lifestyle means a lot of iPhones spend their weekends fishing, walking and birdwatching, so water damage and cracked screens come with the territory. We repair water damage, screens, batteries and cameras, and we handle board-level faults too.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Benoni by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
