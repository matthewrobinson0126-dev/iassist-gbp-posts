Slow iMac, a Mac mini that won't start, or a desktop Mac that has started overheating? If you're in Boksburg, you don't have to load it into the car.

Our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte is about 26 km from Boksburg, roughly 30 minutes' drive. Instead, for a flat R300, we collect your Mac from your door and return it once it's fixed, insured both ways.

We repair at component level in our static-safe lab, from SSD upgrades and cooling services to power and logic board faults. Every job starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty.
