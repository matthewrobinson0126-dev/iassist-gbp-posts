Springs and Brakpan grew up as mining towns, and Springs still has a strong industrial base. It's a working, shift-pattern part of the East Rand.

In many homes the iPhone is a single, hard-used phone that has to keep going. Instead of replacing it, we repair screens, batteries and charging ports, and we fix board-level faults that other shops turn away.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Springs & Brakpan by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
