Linden's 4th Avenue runs on iPhones: food shots, flat-lays and the Instagram Stories that bring people through café doors. When yours cracks, slows down or stops charging, it's a problem.

Linden is one of our closest suburbs. Our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte is about 14 km away, usually a 16 to 20-minute drive.

We repair iPhone screens and batteries, and we do board-level work such as charging IC faults that many repairers send away. Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty. If you can't come to us, we offer courier collection from R300.
