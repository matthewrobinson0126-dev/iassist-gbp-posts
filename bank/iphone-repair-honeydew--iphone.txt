Honeydew sits on the Crocodile Ramble route out to the Cradle of Humankind, and it's also a fast-growing commuter suburb on the N1 Western Bypass.

Between weekend day trips and daily commutes, local iPhones run navigation for hours and charge in the car, which wears out batteries and ports. We repair both, along with screens and board-level faults.

Drop your iPhone off at our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or book door-to-door courier collection from R300.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
