We repair every iPhone, from the iPhone 8 to the 18 Pro Max: screens, batteries, Face ID, charging ports, cameras and logic boards.

Every phone is diagnosed under a microscope, the failed part is named in your quote, and we fix the part, not the whole phone. If a repair isn't worth it, we'll tell you honestly.

Drop it off at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or book our R300 door-to-door collection anywhere in Gauteng. Outside Gauteng, we arrange courier collection nationwide.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
