We repair every standard iPad, from the 5th to the 11th gen.

Glass-only refurbishment is available on all of them, and we also replace batteries, repair Lightning and USB-C charging ports (including the 10th gen), fix logic boards and iPads that won't turn on.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect anywhere in Gauteng for a flat R300, insured both ways.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
