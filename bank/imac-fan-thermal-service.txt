Fan running flat out from start-up, hot rear vents, slowdowns under load, or an iMac that shuts itself off? Dust and dried thermal paste are the usual cause, especially on machines five years or older that have never been serviced.

A full thermal service brings the noise and the heat right down. We also check for fan faults caused by load-shedding damage.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or ask us about door-to-door collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
