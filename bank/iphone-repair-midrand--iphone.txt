We offer same-day iPhone repairs for Midrand customers: screens, batteries, charging IC faults and more, using OEM parts. Our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte is about 35 km away, roughly 30 to 40 minutes via the N1.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you get a clear price first, and repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
