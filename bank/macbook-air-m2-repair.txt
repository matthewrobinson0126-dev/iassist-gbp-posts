We repair MagSafe 3 ports, display backlight faults, Touch ID after a top case replacement, liquid damage, screens, batteries and keyboards. Liquid damage needs quick action, because the SSD is soldered to the board.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect anywhere in Gauteng for a flat R300, insured both ways.
