Not in Gauteng? Your iMac or Mac mini can still come to us.

Whether you're in Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha, Bloemfontein or Polokwane, The Courier Guy collects your Mac from your door, we repair it at our lab in Silvamonte, Johannesburg, and they bring it back to you.

We handle everything from SSD upgrades and slow, overheating Macs to power supply and logic board faults. Every job starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
