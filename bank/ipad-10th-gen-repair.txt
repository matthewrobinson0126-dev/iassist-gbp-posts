Cracked the screen on your iPad 10th gen? You may not need a whole new display.

The 10th gen iPad (2022) brought USB-C, a 10.9-inch screen and Touch ID in the top button. Its glass isn't laminated to the display, so in many cases we can replace the glass only, which costs less than a full screen.

We also repair the soldered USB-C port with micro-soldering, keeping your data in place. That typically costs R1,000 to R1,500.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre, or use our R300 Gauteng collection and delivery. Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty.
