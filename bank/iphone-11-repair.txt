Still using an iPhone 11? At six years old, a worn battery is almost certain, and the Lightning port is often tired after years of daily charging.

At iAssist Repair we know this model inside out. The faults we see most are battery degradation, charging port wear, LCD screen failures, water seals that have aged, rear camera faults and charging IC problems. We fix all of them at our workshop in Silvercrest Shopping Centre, Silvamonte, including board-level charging IC repairs.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know exactly what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty, and we collect across Gauteng for a flat R300.
