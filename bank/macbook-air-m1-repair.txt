The M1 MacBook Air (A2337) is still one of the most popular laptops around.

The faults we see most are liquid damage needing micro-soldering, USB-C ports that won't charge, Touch ID problems after a top case replacement, running hot, and random crashes. We repair screens, batteries and logic boards too.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.
