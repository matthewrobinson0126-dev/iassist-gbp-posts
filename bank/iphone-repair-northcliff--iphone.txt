Northcliff Hill is one of the best places in Johannesburg to photograph the skyline at sunset. It's also loose rock and steep paths, which is how a lot of iPhones end up with cracked screens and scuffed camera lenses.

We repair iPhones for Northcliff customers: screens, batteries, charging faults and board-level repairs such as charging IC work. Our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte is about 15 km away, roughly 18 to 22 minutes' drive. Courier collection is available from R300.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty.
