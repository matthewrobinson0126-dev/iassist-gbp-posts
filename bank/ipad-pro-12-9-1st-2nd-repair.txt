The first and second iPad Pro 12.9-inch (2015-2017) have big batteries that wear with heavy use, and a lot of glass to crack.

We can replace just the cracked glass, fit new batteries, and repair Lightning ports, ProMotion flickering on the 2nd gen and Home buttons, using OEM-spec parts.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
