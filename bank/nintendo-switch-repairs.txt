A Switch lives a harder life than any other console. It gets thrown in school bags, dropped on tiled floors and charged on whatever cable is closest.

So the faults are different too. We see Joy-Cons that drift, cracked screens, consoles that stop charging after a cheap dock, and game cards that suddenly can't be read.

We repair every model at component level: the Switch 2, Switch OLED, original Switch and Switch Lite. Joy-Con drift repair starts from R400 per Joy-Con, and screens start from R950 on the original Switch.

Every Switch gets a free diagnostic, and repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
