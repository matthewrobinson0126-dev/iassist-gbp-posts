Glenvista and Bassonia are two of the most school-dense suburbs in Johannesburg South, with Glenvista High, Glenanda Primary, Mulbarton Primary and more within a few minutes of each other.

That means a lot of first phones taking school-bag knocks. We repair cracked screens, batteries and charging ports at fair prices, and we fix board-level faults too.

Pop in to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our courier collection from R300 and we'll fetch your iPhone from your door.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
