The M4 MacBook Air is still very new, and its Sky Blue colour uses its own display assembly.

We repair screens, batteries, MagSafe 3 and charging ports, and Touch ID after a top case replacement. If it's had liquid on it, don't power it on; bring it straight in. Contact us first so we can confirm parts for this model.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
