The iPhone 12 mini is Apple's most compact modern iPhone, and it has the smallest battery to match. Battery health below 80% is very common on this model now, and you feel it quickly.

We replace iPhone 12 mini batteries with OEM or aftermarket cells, and we also repair OLED screens, cracked back glass, worn Lightning ports, camera issues and Face ID faults.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic at our workshop in Silvercrest Shopping Centre, Silvamonte, so you get a clear price before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty, and we collect across Gauteng for a flat R300.
