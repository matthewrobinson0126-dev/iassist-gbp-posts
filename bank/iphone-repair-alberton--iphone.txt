Alberton is a commuter suburb: most people drive out each weekday to work in Johannesburg, Sandton or the East Rand.

That means iPhones running Maps and traffic apps every day on the M1, often while charging in the car. Batteries and charging ports take the strain. We repair both, as well as screens and board-level faults.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Alberton by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
