Boksburg is one of the East Rand's original mining towns, and today East Rand Mall is the weekend destination for the whole region.

Busy mall trips mean drops in parking lots and knocks from trolleys, so cracked screens are common. We repair screens, back glass, batteries and charging ports, and we handle board-level faults too.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Boksburg by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.
