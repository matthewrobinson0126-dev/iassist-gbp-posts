We repair every iPad Air 11-inch, from the original 2013 Air to the M3 in 2025.

On the Air 1, 2 and 3 we can replace just the glass. From the Air 4 onwards we do full screen replacements. We also repair soldered charging ports on the Air 1 to 6, replace the modular USB-C port on the Air 7 for R2,500, and fix batteries that drain fast.

Visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or use our R300 Gauteng collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
