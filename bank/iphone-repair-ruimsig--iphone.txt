Ruimsig is built around Ruimsig Country Club, a championship course designed by Gary Player and Ron Kirby.

Rangefinder apps, digital scorecards and photos on the course run members' iPhones hard for hours at a time, which wears batteries out fast. We replace batteries and screens, repair charging ports and cameras, and handle board-level faults.

Drop your iPhone off at our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or book door-to-door courier collection from R300.

Every job starts with a free diagnostic and a clear price, and comes with a 6-month warranty.
