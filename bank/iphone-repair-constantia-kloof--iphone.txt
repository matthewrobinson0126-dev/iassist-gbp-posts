Constantia Kloof has Kloofendal Nature Reserve inside the suburb and the Walter Sisulu Botanical Garden a short drive away.

That means a lot of iPhones used for trail photos, birdwatching and Maps on long walks, along with the odd slip on a rocky path. We repair cameras, screens, batteries and charging ports, and we handle board-level faults too.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Constantia Kloof by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
