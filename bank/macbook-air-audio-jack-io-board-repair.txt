Headphones plugged in but no sound? Speakers silent because your MacBook Air still thinks the headphones are connected? Microphone dead on Zoom and Teams?

On a MacBook Air, the I/O board runs the headphone jack and more. We repair or replace it without disturbing the rest of the machine.

A flex cable replacement costs R500 to R900. A full I/O board replacement runs from R900 on older A1466 models up to R2,000 on M2 and M3 models. We cover MacBook Air models from the Intel A1466 through M1, M2, M3, M4 and M5.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic and carries a 6-month warranty.
