The iPhone 11 Pro was Apple's first triple-camera iPhone. Six years on, most are due some attention.

The faults we see most on this model are degraded batteries, OLED burn-in from heavy use, a worn Lightning port and telephoto camera failures. We repair all of them at our workshop in Silvercrest Shopping Centre, Silvamonte.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you get a clear answer and price before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty, and we collect and deliver anywhere in Gauteng for a flat R300.
