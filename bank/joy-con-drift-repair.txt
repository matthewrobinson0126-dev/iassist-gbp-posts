Your character walks off on their own, the camera won't stop turning, or the menu keeps scrolling. That's Joy-Con drift, the most common Switch fault there is.

The stick sensor inside the Joy-Con wears with use and dust gets in, until it reports movement you're not making. We replace the worn stick from R400 per Joy-Con. That's a fraction of the price of a new pair.

To stop it coming back, ask about a Hall Effect upgrade from R650 per Joy-Con. It uses magnets instead of rubbing contacts. Switch Lite built-in sticks start from R450, and we also repair Joy-Con 2 for the Switch 2.

Repairs carry a 6-month warranty. Tap Book to stop the drift.
