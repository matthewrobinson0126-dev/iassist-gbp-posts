Kempton Park is home to OR Tambo International, Africa's second-busiest airport.

Many iPhones here belong to airline crew, ground staff, freight handlers and frequent flyers, and a dead phone can mean a missed shift or flight. We repair screens, batteries and charging faults quickly, and we handle board-level repairs too.

You can visit our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or we collect from Kempton Park by courier from R300 and return your iPhone once it's fixed.

The diagnostic is free, you get the price before we start, and every repair carries a 6-month warranty.
