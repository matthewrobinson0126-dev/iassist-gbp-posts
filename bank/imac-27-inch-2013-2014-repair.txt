The 2013-2014 27-inch iMac was a workhorse, and the first to ship with a Fusion Drive. Many are still going strong.

We repair GPUs, screens, drives and cooling on these models. Swapping the ageing Fusion Drive for a proper SSD makes a huge difference to start-up and everyday speed.

Bring it to our workshop at Silvercrest Shopping Centre in Silvamonte, or ask us about door-to-door collection. We also accept repairs from anywhere in South Africa by courier.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic, so you know what's wrong and what it costs before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty.
