The iPhone 12 Pro Max was the largest iPhone of 2020 and the first with sensor-shift camera stabilisation. At five years old, the battery and the big 6.7-inch OLED are the usual concerns.

Every repair starts with a free diagnostic at our workshop in Silvercrest Shopping Centre, Silvamonte, so you know the cost before any work begins. Repairs carry a 6-month warranty, and we collect across Gauteng for a flat R300.
